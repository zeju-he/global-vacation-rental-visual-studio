Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains how Global Vacation Rental Visual Studio handles information when you enquire about or purchase remote photo editing, video editing and related digital content services.

Information we may receive

  • Contact details and project information you choose to send us.
  • Property photographs, videos, descriptions and instructions supplied for a project.
  • Transaction and payment-status information made available by the payment provider. We do not ask you to send full card details by email.

How information is used

We use information to respond to enquiries, prepare quotes, perform and deliver requested services, provide customer support, maintain business records and meet applicable legal obligations.

Property media and permissions

You must own or have permission to provide the photographs, videos and other materials you submit. We do not publish customer property media in our portfolio or marketing without separate permission. We do not knowingly alter a property's structural features or amenities in a way that misrepresents the actual accommodation.

Service providers and international processing

We may use third-party providers for website hosting, email, file transfer, editing and payment processing. Depending on the provider, information may be processed in countries other than your own. Those providers handle information under their applicable terms and privacy practices.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. We share it only when needed to provide the service, comply with law, protect rights or respond to a lawful request. We retain project and transaction records for as long as reasonably needed for service, accounting and legal purposes.

Your requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, subject to records we must retain by law or for legitimate business purposes.
